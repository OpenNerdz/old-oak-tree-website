Restaurant, parties and functions

Private dining

Our purpose-built restaurant seats up to 50, with open views across the garden and an emphasis on local, seasonal ingredients and traditional pub favourites.

Whether it is a wedding, birthday, christening or special anniversary, we will do our best to cater for your individual requirements. The restaurant can be booked exclusively for up to 50 guests.

Menus can be arranged to suit your budget, from a full sit-down meal to a buffet. Please call us to discuss what you have in mind.

Events through the year

Our Chinese Banquet has run every winter since 2011 and sells out; the next is Sunday 31 January 2027 at 8pm. Christmas party menus run through December and Christmas Day lunch books up well in advance. We also run theme nights and the occasional charity event; see news for what is coming up.
