Totally Yorkshire

Nick, Laurette, Mollie and Elliot welcome you to our freehouse.

Home-cooked food and real ales in a proper village pub.

We source our food locally: Nidderdale poultry, beef and lamb, fresh seafood from Redcar and Whitby, and seasonal vegetables from Hambleton Produce. The bar keeps an ever-changing line of guest ales alongside Hambleton beers brewed down the road at Melmerby.

Alongside the everyday menu there are ever-changing specials boards, sandwiches and light bites over lunch, and a south-facing beer garden for the summer.
